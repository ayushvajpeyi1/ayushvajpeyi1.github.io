Case 01 / Razorpay

Revenue Orchestrator

Product case proposal
Problem & user
Growing mid-market merchants may run recovery agents without a dedicated growth team to test whether those agents work better together. Customer overlap is a hypothesis to validate using agent logs.
Product choice
Start with Cart Recovery and Subscription Recovery. Recommend when to sequence, delay, or suppress existing outreach within merchant-approved settings.
Why AI
A blanket contact rule can suppress useful follow-ups. Predictive models can help identify which customers respond to which agent and timing. Controlled experiments must establish whether that prediction creates incremental value.
Control & tradeoffs
Merchants review evidence and approve cohorts, budgets, and strategies. The system cannot create discounts or expand contact permissions. Inconclusive tests stay inconclusive; underperforming strategies revert.
Measure
Incremental net recovered revenue against independent agents, with complaints, opt-outs, and unauthorised actions as guardrails.
What remains open
Whether overlap materially harms outcomes, whether merchants have sufficient test volume, and whether coordination beats simpler policies.
Case 02 / MakeMyTrip

Trippie Canvas

Team case competition proposal
Problem & user
Travellers save inspiring reels, but trip research remains scattered across apps. Dates, total cost, and whether a destination matches the reel become clear too late.
Product choice
Let users share a reel to MakeMyTrip and turn it into a location card. Use Myra to research budget options, seasonality, reviews, and nearby activities. Keep the trip available until it fits the traveller’s constraints.
Key tradeoff
Capture intent before adding more planning complexity. Show feasibility and explain relevant nudges instead of treating every saved reel as an immediate booking signal.
Measure
Successful location extraction, researched cards revisited, progression to planning, and incremental bookings. Monitor incorrect locations, stale information, and notification opt-outs.
Evidence & limits
The team submission combines research, a survey, prototype concepts, and a financial model. Forecasts are assumptions, not realised revenue. My specific contribution should be documented before making individual ownership claims.
Case 03 / Financial access

Gig-worker credit

Group coursework proposal
Problem & user
Delivery workers with variable income can struggle to present their work history in a form lenders can assess. Platform payouts and bank transactions need context to avoid mistaking transfers for income.
Research direction
The group’s worker interviews indicate documentation friction and a desire for regular work history to count. Direct lender validation remains necessary.
Product choice
A B2B credit-enablement layer that converts permitted platform and financial data into a lender-ready profile. Distinguish verified payouts, bank-derived information, and worker declarations.
Key tradeoff
Improve the evidence available to lenders rather than create a replacement credit score. Show tenure, income variability, low-month earnings, expenses, and obligations without promising approval.
Measure
Assessability and verification time, with approval and repayment performance evaluated against lender policy. Lender willingness to pay remains unvalidated.
